Privacy Policy for ZombieRush

Effective Date: August 14, 2026

ZombieRush ("the Game") is a 2D bullet hell roguelike game developed and published by the developer of ZombieRush.

We respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use ZombieRush.

1. Information We Collect

ZombieRush does not collect, store, or share personal information from users.

The Game does not require users to:

  • Create an account
  • Provide a name or email address
  • Provide a phone number
  • Provide a physical address
  • Provide payment information
  • Sign in through a third-party service

2. Personal Information

ZombieRush does not intentionally collect personally identifiable information such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, or other information that can directly identify you.

3. Device Information

ZombieRush does not intentionally collect or transmit device information, including device identifiers, IP addresses, or precise location information.

4. Advertising and Analytics

ZombieRush does not currently use third-party advertising services or analytics services that collect personal information from users.

5. Children's Privacy

ZombieRush does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Because the Game does not collect personal information, users can play the Game without providing personal information.

6. Third-Party Services

ZombieRush does not currently use third-party services that collect personal information from users.

If third-party services are added in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated to reflect any relevant changes in data collection or processing practices.

7. Data Security

Because ZombieRush does not collect or store personal information, there is no personal information collected by the Game that requires storage or protection.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the Game's features or data practices change.

Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.

9. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or ZombieRush, please contact the developer through the ZombieRush game page on itch.io.
